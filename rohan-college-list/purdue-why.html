Business-School-Specific Traditions & Programs

CareerBound

A formal, points-tracked framework for continuously building professional competencies through coursework, club activities, networking, and campus jobs.

Larsen Leaders Academy

Purdue's flagship business leadership program. Includes Proving Grounds (invitation-only first-year orientation), a residential Learning Community, immersion trips (Gettysburg, NASA, Yosemite, Normandy WWII, Belfast/Titanic), the LAMP mentorship program, the Daniels Leads speaker series, and the Doster Leadership Conference. Requires 10 Leadership Development Points a year to remain in good standing.

Named Case Competitions

Burton D. Morgan Venture Concept Competition · Finance Management Research Challenge (with Purdue Federal Credit Union) · National Women's Case Competition (Apple-sponsored — Purdue won in 2023) · Powershift Case Competition · Global Supply Chain Case Competition · Black MBA National Conference Undergrad Case Competition (Purdue placed 2nd nationally).

Purdue Fintech Conference

Annual event run by the Purdue Fintech Center, with student programming built around fintech careers and technology.

MSA Pathway Program

An accelerated route for undergrads into the Master of Science in Accounting/CPA track, starting as early as sophomore year.

Students manage real university capital — not a simulation — across U.S. equities and fixed income. Why Purdue — Version 1

Purdue's Daniels School of Business is one of the only programs where I could double down on both halves of my resume — the founder who's building Orderly and the sales intern learning fintech at The Founders' Pool — instead of choosing one. Courses like MGMT 47500 (Machine Learning for Business) and MGMT 41220 (FinTech) would let me formalize the AI/ML instincts behind my coding certification, while MGMT 43000 (Professional Selling) would turn what I'm currently learning by trial and error at The Founders' Pool into something structured and repeatable.

Outside the classroom, I want to bring Orderly into the Student Managed Venture Fund and pitch it at the Burton D. Morgan Venture Concept Competition, and I'm drawn to Investment & Trading at Purdue and Boiler Blockchain for the overlap with my internship. The Larsen Leaders Academy's immersion trips — Gettysburg, Normandy — appeal to the same part of me that walks on stage to do stand-up comedy: I want to be tested somewhere the outcome isn't guaranteed.

Purdue is the rare school where my startup, my internship, and my AI background aren't separate interests I have to explain — they're one track, and Daniels is built to run it.

Why Purdue — Version 2

Purdue's Daniels School of Business is one of the only places I wouldn't have to choose between the two threads of my life so far: the founder building Orderly, and the sales intern learning fintech at The Founders' Pool. A course like MGMT 41220 (FinTech) would give language and structure to instincts I've mostly built by doing, and I'd want to bring Orderly into the Student Managed Venture Fund once I've earned my way there.

What pulled me in beyond the classroom is West Lafayette itself. Purdue Research Park is the largest cluster of tech companies in Indiana, and the surrounding Discovery Park District is being built so founders have a reason to stay and keep building after graduation instead of leaving the area — that matters to me in a very literal way. There's also a smaller detail I didn't expect to find: Purdue has an actual Stand-Up Comedy Club. After years of writing sets and reading rooms, finding a school where that's a named campus organization, not just a hobby I do on the side, feels like a sign I'd fit here in more ways than one.
