03 Six weeks, week by week

Nov 1 falls on a Sunday, so treat Friday, Oct 30 as the real cutoff — don't count on a weekend to fix a portal problem. This covers all 29 schools, sequenced by how much writing each one needs rather than by deadline, so essay-heavy schools aren't all crammed into the final days.

04 Verify before you submit

Nine things to double-check directly on each school's site

  1. Penn State — testing: its test-optional policy was stated as covering "through Fall 2026 entry" only; confirm it still applies to your (Fall 2027) cycle before deciding whether to send scores.
  2. Purdue — testing: reporting suggests Purdue reinstated a test-score requirement starting with Fall 2026 entry; confirm whether that still applies to your cycle.
  3. Oregon State — exact dates: the 2026–27 priority deadline wasn't yet posted at research time; confirm once OSU updates its page (should be live by September).
  4. CU Boulder — EA date: CU's own page says Nov 15; some third-party sites say Nov 1. Plan for Nov 1 to be safe, confirm Nov 15 on cuboulder.edu.
  5. LMU — platform: very likely Common App only, but not independently confirmed. Check the school's apply page.
  6. Kelley (Indiana) — supplement: direct-admit applicants historically write no essay beyond the standard Common App one, but this is worth a quick confirm since it can change year to year.
  7. Michigan Ross — portfolio prompt wording: the ~500-word case/artifact requirement is confirmed via Ross's own blog and portfolio subpage, but the live michiganross.umich.edu pages couldn't be directly loaded during research — read the current prompt on-site before drafting.
  8. Michigan State Broad — direct-admit page: confirmed via cached search results, not a direct page load (broad.msu.edu is JS-rendered) — verify the 3.5 GPA auto-consideration threshold on-site.
  9. Pittsburgh — Nov 1 date: a Nov 1 "priority" date circulates on third-party sites, but Pitt's own pages confirm only the Dec 1 scholarship deadline — treat Dec 1 as the real target unless Pitt's own site says otherwise.
